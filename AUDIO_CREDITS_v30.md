# Female scream used for Mina

Source: Female Scream 1 — Nocturnal_Vanguard / AuraVoice
https://opengameart.org/content/female-scream-1
Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/female_scream_1.ogg
License: CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as listed by the uploader. No credit required; source retained here for traceability.

Converted from OGG to mono PCM16 WAV at 44.1kHz, leading silence removed, trimmed to fit the existing automatic-scream cue, loudness normalized and edges faded. Replaces both sfx_mina_fear_scream.wav and the legacy sfx_mina_sob.wav so no older cry remains under that key.

Dream character graphics: user-supplied eight running PNGs and standing PNG. Hidden/caught/crying keys all now map to the same new standing image. Bedroom and waking/phone poses remain outside the dream replacement scope.
